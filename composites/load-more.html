load-more · composite

Append-on-click footer beneath a list/table: optional summary, load-more button (or end marker), optional progress bar. All copy is passed in (i18n-agnostic).

loading=false, done=false — The secondary lg button is enabled. Progress bar shows the current ratio (e.g. 50 of 1,248 loaded).
