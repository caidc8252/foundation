Rich pagination · full bar (rows-per-page + summary + nav)

The complete list/table footer: rows-per-page selector + range summary on the left, simple pagination on the right. The selector is optional (see next example). The summary uses tabular-nums so figures don't reflow on update. Always uses SIMPLE nav (no numbered page buttons) — this is the only allowed pagination in a table/list footer.
