Summary bar · sticky (with scroll demo)

The --sticky variant docks the bar at the top of the nearest scroll ancestor. Use table-frame--flush (overflow:clip — not hidden) so sticky elements propagate up. For a constrained demo the outer wrapper supplies the scroll root; in production the page scroll container does. Pair with data-table--sticky-head; offset thead by 48px (--space-12) so the column header tiles flush below the bar.
