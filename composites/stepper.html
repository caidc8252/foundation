Stepper variants

The numeric stepper — an .input-group composition where the shell owns the border, focus ring, and group chrome, and .stepper__button sizes the −/+ buttons. The first is live: walk it with − / + to see the value clamp and the boundary button dim (aria-disabled) — no separate atMin / atMax rows needed. Below it, the two states you can't reach by clicking: whole-control disabled and invalid (error ring).
