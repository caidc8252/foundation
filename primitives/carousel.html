Carousel · horizontal

A horizontally-scrollable track of equal-width slides using .carousel > .carousel__viewport > .carousel__track > .carousel__item. Navigation via .carousel__prev / .carousel__next buttons and .carousel__dots with .carousel__dot--active.

Carousel · sizes (multiple items per view)

A slide is full-width by default. To show several at once the consumer re-basises each .carousel__item (shadcn basis-1/2 / basis-1/3) — here via inline flex-basis. The baked 16px gutter keeps them apart; Prev/Next advance one slide at a time.

Carousel · spacing (the slide gutter)

The inter-slide gap is a negative margin on .carousel__track (-space-4) paired with matching padding on each .carousel__item (space-4) — netting a clean 16px gutter with no stray leading edge. Change the gap by moving the pair together: below, the default 16px vs a page-local tightened 4px.

Carousel · orientation (vertical)

Add .carousel--vertical to stack the axis: the track flows in a column, the gutter moves to margin-top/padding-top, and the Prev/Next buttons re-pin to top/bottom (chevrons rotate 90°). The viewport takes a fixed height and items basis to show two at a time. ↑/↓ navigate.
