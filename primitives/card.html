Card · sizes

size scales radius and slot padding together: .card--sm (8px radius · 12px padding), .card--md (default — 12px · 20px), .card--lg (16px · 24px). The footer action tracks the card size — sm → btn--sm, md → btn--md, lg → btn--lg.

Medium

.card--md — radius-xl · space-5. The default resting card.

Large

.card--lg — radius-2xl · space-6. Feature panels, hero surfaces.
