Date Picker · interactive

A fully functional date picker: click the trigger to open the calendar popover, use the prev/next buttons to navigate months, click a day to select it (the popover closes and the value updates), and use the clear button to reset. Today's date carries a subtle fill (.calendar__day--today) and the selected day is filled with primary (.calendar__day--selected).
