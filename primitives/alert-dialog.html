Alert Dialog · launch examples

A forced-action confirm dialog. The user must pick Cancel or Confirm — there is no close (×) button and Escape / overlay click do not dismiss. Uses .alert-dialog with .alert-dialog__header (title + optional description) and .alert-dialog__footer (Cancel + Confirm buttons).
