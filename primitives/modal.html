Modal · size presets

Five width presets: .modal--sm (360px), .modal--md (480px, default), .modal--lg (640px), .modal--xl (880px), and .modal--fullscreen (calc(100vw − 64px) × calc(100vh − 64px)). Click a button to open the corresponding modal. Press Esc or click the overlay or close button to dismiss.

Choosing a width

  1. Start at md (480px) — the default; most create/edit forms and detail dialogs belong here.
  2. Width tracks horizontal content shape, never height or importance: the body scrolls, so a tall form is never a reason to widen. Widen only for real horizontal need (side-by-side columns, a table).
  3. Escalate one step at a time — don't jump to xl for a three-field form.
  4. A destructive confirm → sm, and prefer an AlertDialog over a hardened Modal.

Modal · anatomy

The modal shell is composed of an outer scrim (.modal-overlay) and a panel (.modal) with three optional interior slots: .modal__header (title + description + close), .modal__body (scrolls), and .modal__footer (actions).
