Scroll Area · popups & overflow

The viewport's overflow: auto clips floating popups. A dropdown / menu / popover anchored inside the viewport is cut off at its edge; lift the popup out of the overflow ancestor (or use a fixed surface like a modal) and it shows in full. This is the general rule in governance principle 17 — it holds for any overflow container (.table-scroll, an overflow:auto card), not just this one.
