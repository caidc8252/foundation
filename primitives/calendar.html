Calendar · single select

The default calendar — no footer, matching shadcn. Just .calendar__header (nav buttons + caption) over .calendar__grid (weekdays + days). Day states use .calendar__day--today, --selected, --outside, --disabled. (Today / Clear or preset shortcuts are opt-in — see the presets example below.)

Calendar · dropdown caption

The dropdown caption layout: the month/year label becomes two borderless .calendar__dropdown triggers (each a <select> + chevron), so you can jump straight to any month or year instead of stepping one month at a time. Prev / next still step a month and keep both dropdowns in sync.

Calendar · range selection

A range spans months, so it's shown as two months side by side (place two .calendar in a flex row). Range state classes: .calendar__day--range-start, .calendar__day--range-middle, .calendar__day--range-end — endpoints are a primary-700 pill on the surface-3 track. Click a start day, then an end day.

Calendar · presets

Quick-set shortcuts in a card footer (shadcn's Presets example): the calendar over a bordered-top row of .btn .btn--secondary .btn--sm buttons. Each jumps the selection to a relative day (Today, Tomorrow, …).

Calendar · date and time picker
